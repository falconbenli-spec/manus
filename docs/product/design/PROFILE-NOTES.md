# 3,6T company profile (Feb 2026, Arabic): visual notes for the platform

Source: the owner's `3,6T Profile 2026Feb Ar URLD.pdf`, 62 landscape pages, rendered page by page (PDFKit) on 19 Sep 2026. These notes cover the visual language only: no client figures and no names of people. Portfolio work belongs to the clients, and the platform does not reproduce it.

## 1. The company's own key visual: «قاعة الضوء» (Hall of Light)
Used on the cover, every section divider, the contact page and the closing page (p01, p03, p08, p09, p36, p51, p55, p61, p62).
- **Space:** a vast dark hall in deep teal-green, with volumetric fog and a wet, reflective black floor.
- **Monoliths:** a forest of tall, thin, translucent glass slabs, glowing teal at their edges, at varied depths. Some slabs carry imagery (cover and closing page: faces, architecture, a digital human).
- **Figure:** a single small figure in a thobe stands among them, seen from behind or in profile. That gives a heroic sense of scale.
- **Lighting:** cold teal key light from behind the slabs, light shafts, bloom on the slab edges, and near-black between them.
- **Palette of the scene:** black, then deep teal (≈#03201C–#0B3B34), then brand turquoise, then a bright mint glow on the edges. No other hues.

→ This is the brand's own answer to Dala's brain, and it is the strongest candidate for the platform hero.

## 2. Colour in use
- **Brand turquoise:** titles such as «وُلدت عظيمة» and the section tab bars.
- **Vivid mint green:** a brighter tint of the turquoise, on every headline figure (+19.6M, +3.14B …) over charcoal, and on the gradient section bars «نماذج الأعمال».
- **Charcoal panels** (≈#2E3338) carry the figures. Light grey panels (≈#EBEBEB) carry the body copy.
- **White:** thick white frames around full-bleed imagery.
- **No yellow, violet, orange or pink** appear as brand accents. Those colours appear only inside client logos and photos.

## 3. Shape language
- **The slant:** title tabs are parallelograms with one slanted end (the comma's angle), in the top corners of pages. The bottom-left logo tab is a dark slanted bar.
- **White frames** with a slanted notch where the title tab cuts in.
- **Headings:** heavy Arabic display type with a thick underline bar («أرقامنا تتكلم», «مشاريعنا», «قيمنا»).
- **Values** are numbered 1–5 with big ghosted numerals: الإنجاز، الشمولية، الإبداع، الجودة، الاعتزاز. These are already on the login screen.

## 4. What the platform takes from this
1. **A seventh form, «أعمدة» (pillars):** the Hall of Light rebuilt from the brand comma lattice. Tall slabs, each a vertical sheet of the pattern, stand at depth on a reflective floor, with teal fog and bright mint edges, and a slow camera drift through them. It suits the login and the section openings. **No human figure is rendered**, because the platform is for employees and must not invent people.
2. **Palette for every hero form:** deep teal → turquoise → mint glow → sky/white highlights, on black. This matches the owner's instruction of 19 Sep 2026 to drop yellow and violet.
3. **Figures:** «أرقام» (big figures) render in the mint tint, as in the profile.
4. **Slanted tab** as the eyebrow shape: optional and restrained.
