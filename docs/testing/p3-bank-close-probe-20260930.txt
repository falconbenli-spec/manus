Reading:
  G1  no ledger kind reads a classified bank line and the ledger has no bank-charge or interest purpose. Before the charge is
      posted the reconciliation balances (unmatched bank −2500, book 1,000,000, difference 0); after a charge journal is posted
      in the GL the classified line is still subtracted as unmatched bank, so the difference becomes exactly the charge (2500).
  G2  matching is one line to one record at the same amount: a 1,500.00 bulk transfer cannot be matched to its two custody
      records (amount_mismatch), a posted manual journal on the bank account cannot be matched at all (kind), advance
      receipts are refused as a kind, and the bank_matches CHECK allows six kinds only.
  G3  a returned supplier payment is recorded (payment_returns has the row) but cashRecords does not list it — nor the advance
      receipt — and supplier_payment_return is not a ledger kind.
  G4  the ledger period never reopens: finance refuses reopen (transition_denied), the finance_period_identity trigger refuses a
      direct update, and the checklist's approved reopen leaves the month open with ledger_locked=true and the finance period
      closed, so an adjustment dated inside the month is refused with period_closed.
  G5  approve_close approves a month holding an unposted journal and an active bank account with no approved reconciliation;
      the close period carries no system checks at all.
  G6  an approved amortization entry reports posting_status 'not_posted' because entryView writes the constant, and no ledger
      kind exists for amortization entries.
