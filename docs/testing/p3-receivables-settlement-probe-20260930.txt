Reading:
  G1  No module in app/ writes ar_adjustments, ar_disputes or ar_promises, and no route names them: the only exports are
      listReceivables, createClaim, claimAction, recordReceipt, getReceipt, receiptAction. After a confirmed receipt no
      person is offered any action on it, so a bounced cheque or a returned transfer cannot be recorded. The schema does
      allow flipping the receipt row to 'reversed' (ar_receipt_identity, migration 012), but doing so drops the receipt from
      its sub-ledger while its posted journal stays: the receivable control shows 115.00 unexplained, reason no_document.
      That is why the reversal is built as its own record and the receipt row is never edited.
  G2  A direct UPDATE cancels an approved, invoiced and collected claim with no error: no independent approval and no check
      of what stands on the claim.
  G3  The claim view reads disputes only as a boolean («disputed»); promises are read only by app/cash-forecast.mjs. Both
      tables are populated in the tree only by tests inserting rows.
  G4  One transfer for two claims cannot be recorded: the whole amount on the first claim is over_allocation, and the rest
      on the second claim under the same bank reference is duplicate_receipt_reference. ar_receipts.claim_id is NOT NULL
      and no table holds cash without a claim, so a payment that arrives before its claim has nowhere to go.
  G5  Nuance on the inventory: the invoices API already carries reporting_status = 'not_reported' — a column constant
      (CHECK) that reads the same on a draft that was never issued. There is no internal/issued-internally label, no
      simulation flag, and no link to the e-invoicing queue, whose row can be moved to «accepted» by an injected provider
      (tests/einvoice-gateway.test.mjs does exactly that) while nothing is connected.
  G6  Every reader that equates «confirmed» with collected would keep counting a reversed receipt: project-axes (collection
      axis, refunds and the closure checklist), cash-forecast, reports R31 and R01, the ledger customer statement, and bank
      matching candidates. Of these, only app/project-axes.mjs is in this package's scope; the others are listed in the
      final report for their owners.
